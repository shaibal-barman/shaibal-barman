## Selected work

### [Customer Lifecycle Management Framework](https://github.com/shaibal-barman/customer-lifecycle-management)

A practical working system for keeping customer context, health evidence, risk, enablement, value, and follow-through visible as a portfolio grows.

The repository includes:

- an evidence-based account health and prioritization model;
- an early-risk response procedure;
- adoption, enablement, Voice of Customer, and value-review guidance;
- reusable account, feedback, and value-review templates; and
- worked examples showing how customer risk and feedback become owned action.

## How I approach the work

1. **Understand the operation before recommending an action.**
2. **Separate evidence, interpretation, and missing context.**
3. **Keep one accountable owner visible to the customer.**
4. **Connect activity to an outcome the customer recognizes.**
5. **Use AI to improve preparation, never to replace judgment.**
